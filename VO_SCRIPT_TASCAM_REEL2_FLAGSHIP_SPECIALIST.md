### Tone (Stage 9) — The Integration Authority

Authoritative and broad in perspective, prioritising ecosystem mastery and workflow
synchronisation. Chosen because both units in this reel are defined by what they command
outside themselves — a DAW timeline, a rack of sequencers, a broadcast call. Stage 9's sample
passage for this direction is written about exactly that: HUI and MCU emulation alongside
hardwired MIDI time code, holding an ecosystem in alignment.

**Delivery.** ~140 wpm. Assured rather than emphatic.

### Content rules observed

Zero pricing. No other-brand comparison. Every figure from a Stage 8 master table. The reel's
framing beat states explicitly that these two units share **no architectural tier** — they are
paired on narrative weight, not architecture — and the script must never imply otherwise.
