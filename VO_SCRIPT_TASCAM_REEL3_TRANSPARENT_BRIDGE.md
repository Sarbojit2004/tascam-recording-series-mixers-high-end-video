**[00:00 – 00:10] · Hook · "NO PREAMPS"**
> This unit has no microphone preamplifiers. That is not a limitation. It is the specification.

**[00:10 – 00:30] · "YOU ALREADY OWN THE DESK"**
> There is a class of engineer this is built for. The console is world-class. The outboard
> preamplifiers are chosen, matched and paid for. What is missing is twenty-four track capture
> and DAW transport — without buying another set of preamplifiers to get them.

**[00:30 – 00:46] · "NOT A CONSOLE"**
> So the TASCAM Studio Bridge is not a console. No preamplifiers. No faders. No channel strips.
> No summing bus. A twenty-four in, twenty-four out line-level bridge, and nothing else.

**[00:46 – 01:18] · DB25 Injection graphic**
> Twenty-four lanes of line-level audio leave the existing desk and converge on high-density
> DB25 connectors, compliant with AES fifty-nine, two thousand and twelve. Inside the housing
> there is no amplification stage, no equaliser and no summing bus, so there is nothing in the
> path that could impose a character of its own. The response through it is flat. What arrives
> at the workstation over USB is what the desk sent.

**[01:18 – 01:30] · "HUI/MCU EMULATION"**
> Transport is commanded from the rack. Record, play, stop and record-arm, over HUI and MCU.

**[01:30 – 01:46] · Verified specification**
> Total harmonic distortion plus noise, at or below zero point zero zero three percent at one
> kilohertz. Dynamic range, one hundred decibels or better. Input impedance, ten kilohms or
> greater. Output, two hundred ohms.

**[01:46 – 02:04] · "WITH OR WITHOUT A DAW"**
> The same twenty-four track engine serves three configurations. A hosted session. A capture
> with no computer present at all. Or an outboard preamplifier rack feeding it directly.

**[02:04 – 02:16] · "24 × 24 DB25"**
> Six multi-pin connectors carrying twenty-four inputs and twenty-four outputs — in place of
> forty-eight discrete cables.

**[02:16 – 02:32] · "MIDI · MTC · SPP"**
> Five-pin DIN MIDI in and out. MIDI Time Code. Song Position Pointer. Twenty watts, four
> point five kilograms, six-U rack-mountable.

**[02:32 – 02:46] · "MODERNISED, NOT REPLACED"**
> The desk keeps its signal path. The room gains twenty-four track capture, USB interfacing
> and transport control. Nothing is traded for it.

**[02:46 – 02:58] · Resolution screen**
> Shivansh Electronics. Professional Audio Infrastructure. Shivansh electronics dot in.
> Call or WhatsApp: plus nine one, nine eight three one six, six two four five eight.
