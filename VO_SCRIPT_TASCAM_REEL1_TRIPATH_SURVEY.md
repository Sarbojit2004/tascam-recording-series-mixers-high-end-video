### Tone (Stage 9) — The Systems Architect

Calculated and methodical, prioritising structural logic and acoustic physics over feature
listing. Chosen because this reel's subject *is* the architecture: the signal-splitting
mechanism that unifies four consoles across three tiers. The Calibration Specialist would be
too narrow for a survey, and the Integration Authority's ecosystem framing belongs to Reel 2.

**Delivery.** ~140 wpm, unhurried despite the reel's pace. The pictures move quickly; the
voice does not chase them.

### Content rules observed

Zero pricing. No other-brand comparison. Every figure from a Stage 8 master table. The Model
16's fader travel is UNVERIFIED and is never stated. The Model 16 and Model 24 are never
credited with MIDI or DAW transport.
